# AI Ocean UXMM 2026

## About UXMM

UXMM is a volunteer-led organization supporting Myanmar’s design and digital product community. Since 2020, it has connected local practitioners with international design practices through education, community activities, and industry partnerships.

## Core values

- **Community:** Support Myanmar’s design community.
- **Learning:** Make quality design education accessible.
- **Innovation:** Encourage learning and growth.
- **Collaboration:** Work together through teams and partnerships.
- **Inclusivity:** Welcome different perspectives and widen access to opportunities.
- **Impact:** Create lasting value through community initiatives.
